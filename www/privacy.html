Cape Hardware Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Cape Hardware is a single-player trading game. This policy explains what information the game and the services it uses collect, and why.

Information the game keeps

Your saved game, hall of fame scores and settings (such as sound on or off) are stored only on your own device. We have no accounts, no servers and no way to see this information. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with ads. Google uses this as described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings under Google, then Ads. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads.

The full version shows no ads.

Purchases

The full version is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details are never shared with us. The app only receives confirmation that your Google account owns the full version, so it can unlock it and restore it later.

Children

Cape Hardware is not designed for children under 13. The game deals with crime, parole and corruption as satire, and is rated for older players.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal information ourselves, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For information Google collects through ads or Google Play, use your Google account settings or contact Google.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
