## Full description (max 4,000 characters)

Seven years in Pollsmoor. The house is gone, the BMW belongs to the state, and the old crew is dead, inside or pretending they never knew you. You walk out with a tired Hilux bakkie, R5,000 from your mother and a R10,000 debt to Oom Sakkie that grows 6% every day.

Your only skills come from your old life. Now you have to use them the legal way.

BUY LOW, SELL HIGH, ACROSS THE CAPE
Trade cement, pine, balau decking, fibre cement boards, IBR sheets, steel tube, roof paint and solar panels between Bellville, Epping, Mitchells Plain, Somerset West, Stellenbosch, Paarl and Atlantis. Every town has goods it sells cheap and goods it pays well for. Learn the routes, watch for load shedding and steel strikes, and get there before the price moves.

PLAY IT ON YOUR HUSTLER'S PHONE
The whole game runs on your character's phone. Check prices in the Trade app, plan routes in Maps, pay Sakkie in the Bank app and keep up with the Cape Wire news feed. Work, tips and trouble arrive as messages.

GROW FROM A BAKKIE TO AN EMPIRE
- Trade up from the Hilux to a 4-ton, an 8-ton flatbed, a crane truck and finally a Superlink
- Buy a yard, hire a crew to sell to walk-in builders and a driver to deliver and collect loads
- Fit alarms, trackers, dogs, CCTV and night guards to keep thieves off your stock

THE OLD LIFE KEEPS CALLING. THIS IS CAPE TOWN, AFTER ALL.
Old friends with "one parcel". Copper cable that came off a substation. A perlemoen run out of Hangklip. A roadblock officer who wants "something for a cooldrink". A municipal tender with a kickback. Gang money to wash through your books. Dirty deals pay big, but every one raises your parole heat. Let it get too hot and Mrs Jacobs sends you back inside.

FREE TO PLAY
Play the 30-day hustle free, with one ad at the end of each run. Get the full version once-off to unlock the 60-day empire mode, remove all ads and get bonus perks.

Cape Hardware is a satire of crime and redemption. It does not encourage any illegal activity.

Check Google's current AdMob data disclosure guide when filling this in, because Google updates it.
